# Stakeholder Interview Guide

Use this to fill the **[FILL FROM INTERVIEWS]** items in the BRD. Spend 15 to 20 minutes per person, take notes in their own words and record real numbers.

## Requesters
1. Last time something broke, how did you report it? How long until someone responded?
2. Did you have to follow up? How many times?
3. What information did you wish you had given, or been asked for?
4. How do you know when it is fixed?

## Supervisor
1. Walk me through the last request you handled, from arrival to closure.
2. How do you decide what is urgent? Which words or situations always escalate?
3. How do you choose who to send? What goes wrong?
4. Which jobs need your approval today, and why? Is there a cost limit?
5. How many requests come in per day? How many hours go on sorting and assigning?
6. Which problems keep coming back?

## Technician
1. How do you receive jobs now? What is missing from them?
2. How often are you sent to the wrong kind of job?
3. What happens when you cannot finish a job?

## Manager
1. Which numbers do you see today, and how often? Which do you wish you saw?
2. What are the SLAs, formal or informal? How often are they missed?
3. Which assets or places cost the most in repeat repairs?
